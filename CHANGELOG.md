# Changelog

All notable public releases of Howling Werewolf are documented here.

## 1.0.0 — First stable release

- Completed and validated the full survival progression from infection through the Alpha Trial and endgame exploration.
- Added three playable forms: normal Werewolf, quadruped wolf, and trial-unlocked Beast.
- Added werewolf levels, tree skills, active/passive abilities, claw combat, lunar transformation, and persistent player progression.
- Added Silver equipment, Wolfsbane, opposing potions, Hunters, renewable village patrols, and full-moon Feral Werewolf encounters.
- Added the complete single-player Alpha Trial with ritual setup, empowered Hunters, a multi-stage Alpha boss, revival behavior, and repeat rewards.
- Corrected wild Hunter spawning, village Hunter replacement, werewolf elytra alignment, and the quadruped tail center.
- Clarified that Armored Instinct counts only equipment with positive armor value.
- Clarified the Alpha Werewolf Badge's separate hotbar XP and transformed fatal-hit protection requirements.
- Added English and Simplified Chinese localization, configurable progression/world rules, public build files, and consistent MPL-2.0 code and CC-BY-SA-4.0 art licensing metadata.
