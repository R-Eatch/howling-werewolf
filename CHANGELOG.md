# Changelog

All notable public releases of Howling Werewolf are documented here.

## Howling Werewolf 1.0.0

**Howling Werewolf 1.0.0 is the first public stable release of the project.**

- Features a complete survival progression from initial infection and awakening through werewolf growth, the Alpha Trial, and endgame exploration.
- Includes three playable werewolf forms: Werewolf, Quadruped Wolf, and the trial-unlocked Beast Form.
- Includes werewolf levels, two progression systems, active and passive abilities, claw combat, lunar transformations, and persistent player progression.
- Adds Silver equipment, Wolfsbane, opposing potions, Hunters, village Hunter patrols, and full-moon Feral Werewolf encounters.
- Includes the complete single-player Alpha Trial with the Moon-Oath ritual, empowered Hunters, a multi-stage Alpha boss fight, and repeatable rewards.
- Includes configurable progression and world rules.
- Includes full English and Simplified Chinese localization.
- Source code is released under **MPL-2.0**, while original art assets are licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
